# Our Block · 优城美市 — grounding doc for the contextual help assistant

This is the *why* behind the app. Use it to ground "why is this here / why is it designed
this way" questions. Do not invent UI — the on-screen snippet is authoritative for *what* is
present; this doc explains *purpose*.

## What Our Block is
A place-based civic wellbeing app. Neighbours notice the **good** in their block and flag what
needs **care**, then act. The core belief: wellbeing is built by small, visible, local acts —
and every problem should stay coupled to a concrete way to act on it ("learned hopefulness").

## The two streams
- **Good stream** — savoring. Capture and share the good already happening (a bloom, a mural, a
  neighbour's kindness). The Good stream is for appreciation; it never escalates to the city.
- **Care stream** — things that need attention (a broken light, litter, a hazard). Each care item
  is routed to the **lowest viable rung** of action.

## The action ladder (the AI's job)
Every observation is routed to the smallest action that can resolve it:
1. **self** — small enough to tend yourself; share so others can savour or join.
2. **rally** — neighbours can take it on together; rally a few people nearby.
3. **city** — needs an agency; a report is drafted for the responsible department to send.
The AI suggests a rung and a short reason; the person always decides.

## Capitalization & efficacy (why the panels exist)
- **My Impact** makes a person's own acts visible (intrapersonal self-efficacy) — effort you can see.
- **Our block this month** shows collective efficacy — what the neighbourhood did together.
- **Active-constructive responding**: cheering and engaging with others' good news amplifies it.
- The **Loop** tracks a care item from captured → rallied/reported → resolved, so action feels
  completable rather than endless.

## Reactions
- **Cheer** (👏) — celebrate / savour.
- **Me too** — signal shared experience or support.
- **Help** — offer to pitch in on a care item.

## Stance
The app offers; people decide. Suggestions (including AI ones) are to **adapt, edit, or ignore** —
never an obligation. The help assistant is a friendly tour guide, not a coach or evaluator: it
explains what's on screen and the smallest useful next step, and never does the user's work for them.
