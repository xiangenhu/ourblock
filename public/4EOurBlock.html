Start where you stand

You already know your block.

The corner that blooms in spring. The light that's been out for weeks. The bench where the elders sit. You notice these things every single day — because this is where your life actually happens.

The question was never whether you care. It's whether caring ever turns into change.

Why it usually stalls

Noticing, with nowhere to go.

Most apps only collect complaints. The good goes uncelebrated, and the fixable feels like someone else's job. Over time, that quiet helplessness is what wears a neighbourhood — and a person — down.

Three reasons your block matters

A better block is a better body, mind & life together.

BodyA place that keeps you wellCleaner, safer, greener streets. Walkable corners, working lights, tended parks — the physical block your health lives in.
MindA place that lifts youBelonging, pride and agency instead of helplessness. Somewhere to savour the good, not only stew on the broken.
Loved onesA place that holds your peopleKids, elders, friends and neighbours — a block that looks out for each other is the one you want them living in.

Two feeds, two kinds of care

Savour the good. Act on what needs care.

The Good ✦

A space to notice and celebrate what neighbours do well. It never escalates — it just makes the good visible, and lifts the mind of everyone who sees it.

Needs Care △

Notice what needs attention, then move it through a visible loop — report, handling, a verified fix — so the body of the block actually improves.

How Enact stays doable

Routed to the lowest viable rung.

Our Block suggests the smallest action that can actually resolve it — so change feels possible, not overwhelming.

Under the simplicity

Notice → Route → Act. One loop.

A friendly AI reads each observation and points to the right rung — and if there's no AI configured at all, a transparent rule still keeps everything working.
